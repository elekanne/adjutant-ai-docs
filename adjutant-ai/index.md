# Adjutant AI

Adjutant AI is an agentic AI platform for Splunk that runs entirely inside your
own deployment, so no data leaves your environment. It answers questions about
your environment, builds searches and dashboards, and works through multi-step
tasks against the indexes and knowledge objects you already have.

- [User guide](./user) covers day to day use: asking questions, working with
  results, and the interactive UI.
- [Administration](./admin) covers installation, configuration, access control
  and running the platform.
